Daily Update Wallpaper

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy describes Daily Update Wallpaper for Windows, published by RedRain, and this website. It explains wallpaper requests, information stored on your device, and diagnostic telemetry. Behavior can vary between app versions.

Wallpaper services

The app connects to Microsoft's Bing services to request daily image information and download wallpaper images. Requests use language and market preferences based on Windows language settings and, where used, the configured home region. These preferences are not GPS coordinates.

Bing receives ordinary network request information, including your IP address and the requested image or service URL. These requests are necessary to obtain wallpaper content from Bing. The app does not require an app account or a sign-in to a wallpaper service.

Information on your device

The app stores preferences and wallpaper information, including image URLs and file names, in local application settings. It downloads and caches wallpaper files locally and can remove older cached images during cleanup. Images you choose to export are saved to the folder you select.

Local storage is separate from diagnostic telemetry. Saving a wallpaper locally does not mean that all information processed by the app stays on your device.

Diagnostic and usage telemetry

The app's diagnostic implementation uses Microsoft Application Insights to report usage and reliability information. App versions containing this implementation can send:

  • A persistent device-derived identifier and a session identifier.
  • Operating-system and app versions.
  • Interaction events, page views, metrics, and exceptions.
  • HTTP request diagnostics, such as the URL, host, duration, and result or status code.
  • Language information and response content recorded when parsing a wallpaper-service response fails.

This information supports diagnostics and understanding how the app operates. It should not be assumed to be anonymous: identifiers, request information, and error details can distinguish a device or session. Delivery depends on the app version, network availability, and the diagnostic service.

This policy does not promise a fixed telemetry retention period, an in-app opt-out, or automatic deletion of previously transmitted information. Contact support for questions about diagnostic data, retention, or deletion requests.

Microsoft services and downloads

Microsoft operates Bing, Application Insights, and Microsoft Store. Those services may process request and service information under their applicable terms and privacy practices. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft Store downloads and installations are handled by Microsoft separately from this website.

This website and support

This static website has no analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms added by RedRain. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process visitor request information, including IP addresses. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords, credentials, or unnecessary private information.

Your choices and contact

You can uninstall the app if you no longer want it to make wallpaper or diagnostic requests. This does not remove information already received by service providers or images you exported to a chosen folder.

For privacy questions or requests, contact arisewallpaper@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.
